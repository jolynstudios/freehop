With a classic TURN setup, the calls that cannot connect directly flow through a relay that you rent, and you pay for every gigabyte. With Freehop, your servers carry sealed signalling only. The relayed share does not disappear: it rides machines that belong to the session instead.

## How big is the relayed share?

Two public measurements give an idea of how many WebRTC calls need a relay:

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| callstats.io, January 2015 to February 2016 ([The Big Churn: learning from real usage stats](https://webrtchacks.com/usage-stats/), Lasse Lumiaho and Varun Singh, webrtcHacks, April 2016) | 22% of conferences needed some kind of TURN relay server |
| appear.in peer-to-peer calls in Chrome ([What kind of TURN server is being used?](https://medium.com/@fippo/what-kind-of-turn-server-is-being-used-d67dbfc2ff5d), Philipp Hancke, 2017) | 17.7% of connected calls were relayed: 12.1% over TURN/UDP, 5% over TURN/TCP and about 0.5% over TURN/TLS |

Both are older measurements from specific services, and network mixes change. Use them as a starting point, not as a prediction for your users.

## What does relayed media cost?

Relay pricing varies by provider. As one public example, Cloudflare's Realtime TURN service lists **$0.05 per real-time GB** sent from Cloudflare to the TURN client, and is free when used together with Cloudflare's Realtime SFU ([pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/turn/)). Treat it as an example rate and put your own in the calculator.

## Reading the numbers

The arithmetic is simple enough to check by hand. With Freehop's default bitrates, and decimal units (1 GB = 1,000 MB):

| Item | Size |
|---|---|
| One person-hour of relayed audio at 32 kbit/s, delivered by the relay | 14.4 MB |
| One person-hour of relayed audio plus video at 32 + 300 kbit/s | 149.4 MB |
| One call setup through a Freehop gate, per pair of peers | about 15 to 35 KB |

So one hour of one person's relayed audio moves as many bytes as roughly 400 to 1,000 call setups through a gate. At the example rate of $0.05 per GB, 100,000 relayed person-hours a month cost about **$72** for audio only and about **$747** with video, for two-person calls. In a group call, each relayed person receives one stream per other participant, so the relay delivers proportionally more.

The gate's share stays small because connected pairs move signalling onto their own data channels. Keepalives, newcomer discovery and recovery still contribute traffic. In the [qualification lab](../results.mdx), a gate's total for a whole scenario run, retries included, was 30 to 210 KB.

## Who carries the relayed share instead

| Path | Who carries the media | Who pays for the bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| `direct` | Nobody in between | The two people talking, on their own connections |
| `gateway` | A desktop participant's own gateway | That participant's upload |
| `relay` | The gateway of another session member | That member: a desktop participant, or the machine hosting the session |
| `bridged` | A participant who forwards | That participant's upload, plus CPU to re-encode |
| `unreachable` | Nobody | Nobody: no media flows, and Freehop says so |

None of these rows is your operator infrastructure. That is the point of the design, and it comes with obligations:

- **Do not host sessions on servers whose bandwidth you pay for.** A host node relays media for its session. If that host is your own server, the relay bill is back.
- **Forwarding costs a participant's upload.** How much a forwarding participant on home broadband can spare is not yet measured. Freehop caps forwarded video at 200 kbit/s.
- **Gateways have quotas.** By default a gateway relays at most 4 Mbit/s per allocation, 20 Mbit/s per room and 40 Mbit/s in total, separately per direction, with at most 6 allocations per username, 32 per room and 64 overall.

## What you still run

You still run gates, or none at all: a public WebTorrent tracker can serve as a room's only gate, as the [live call](/demo) on this site does. A gate needs no TURN ports, no media bandwidth and a public HTTPS/WebSocket endpoint, plus a UDP port if you enable STUN. See [Gate service](../sdk/gate-service.mdx).
