## What "no warranty" means in practice

Both licenses already exclude warranties and limit liability (Apache-2.0 sections 7 and 8, CC BY 4.0 section 5). For an alpha networking SDK, that has concrete consequences:

- **Test it on your own users' networks.** Freehop's developer ran simulated-NAT tests in a home lab; this was not independent lab testing. Physical home routers, carrier networks, corporate networks and mobile browsers are [not yet verified](./limits.mdx#not-yet-verified).
- **There is no service level.** Nobody guarantees that a gate, a public tracker, a gateway or a host node is available, or that a call connects.
- **Direct paths expose network addresses** to other participants. Say so in your privacy information.
- **Gateways and host nodes spend someone's bandwidth.** If your users run them, make it their informed choice.
- **You are responsible for your deployment:** your backend's authentication, how you deliver tickets, your gate's configuration and your users' data.

Report problems on [GitHub](https://github.com/jolynstudios/freehop/issues).
